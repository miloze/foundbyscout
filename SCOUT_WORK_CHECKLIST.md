### Qualification on the frame-sizing diagnosis

**Confirmed by measurement:** the homepage hero and the park viewer used different sizing rules — width-driven `max(420px, --vw × 9/16)` against height-driven `height: 78vh` — and disagreed by 75px at 1453×941, moving opposite ways as the window changed shape. That is the whole of the diagnosis, and it is the whole of what the fix addresses.

**Withdrawn:** the earlier claim that toolbar movement "follows from `vh`". Modern Safari generally resolves `vh` against the *large* viewport height, so it does not track the toolbars, and no visible resizing was observed on a device in this session. The claim should not have been made.

**On `100svh` in the shared rule:** it caps the frame's *preferred* size against the window. It is not a guarantee that content never exceeds the viewport — the 420px floor and the content minimums still win where they are larger, and a short window can still require scrolling. It is a cap, nothing more.
